# Shotgun Ninjas / Village brand direction

Owner-review proposal, 2026-10-14. Source base: 785788f, shotgunsensei/Shotgun-Ninja-Village. No deployment or catalog publication.

## Core identity

Anime field journal meets action-game armory. Preserve Shotgun Ninjas, Kage-9 / Hayaku Kageru, the signal war, the recovered transmissions, and Ronin Supply. Inked silhouettes, rough-edged cel shading, paper/bone negative space, strong red armor and cyan energy. Classic Shinobi and Kung-Fu inform genre pacing only; no copied characters, logos, art, audio, levels, or names.

Kage-9 canonical identity: athletic cyber-ninja, short textured brown hair with blond-tipped highlight, red mask and plates over gunmetal/black layers, cyan eyes and circular chest core, armored headset, back/hip shotgun silhouettes and energy katana. New village key art is an atmospheric concept rendering, not a replacement character model. Existing canonical dossier art is retained. No rigged or animated 3D character exists in these assets.

## Palette and typography

Ink #111316; charcoal #1b1f24; bone #f2eadc; signal red #e54444; ice #9edce6; muted silver #b5b7bc. Teko condensed display headings and Space Grotesk readable body continue the established brand; system monospace is for short field labels, not long copy. Strong contrast, visible focus, reduced motion. No borrowed Japanese lettering or ornamental pseudo-language.

## Emblem and world system

The original split-ring signal crest contains opposing ring arcs and two central slashes. Four archetype emblems extend the existing alignment quiz without changing scoring:

- Forge Ghost / builder: diamond and recoverable core; Forge Grid.
- Heavy Vanguard / protector: split shield; Operator Havens.
- Signal Hunter / tracer: radar arcs and centered signal; Archive Fractures.
- System Breaker / breaker: fractured polygon/bolt; Noise Sectors.

The village is a layered settlement of repair shops, safehouses, lanterns, rooftops, bridges, and archive towers. Regions keep their existing names. The four marks denote operator archetypes, not newly invented political lore.

## Merch families

Eight existing records, handles, SKUs, prices and options remain authoritative and unchanged. Concepts are separate presentation assets:
Signal Essentials (tee/cap); Kage-9 (hoodie); Transmission Archive (Fracture Scan tee/Forge Protocol long sleeve); Village Marks (stickers); Founders (tee); World Prints (Noise Sector poster).

Generated garment images are visual concepts, not actual product photographs. Live Shopify imagery always takes precedence. A live product lacking imagery is not given an invented garment image. Demo catalog checkout remains disabled; no fulfillment provider is claimed.

## Game implementation handoff

Use the same palette, canonical Kage-9 features, district names and archetype marks. Silhouettes must read at playfield scale: red upper body, cyan core, dark limbs, blond hair crest, compact weapon forms. Keep the face/mask and silhouette consistent in any model turnaround before animation or rigging. Key art is mood/reference only, not a sprite sheet or model.

UI: compressed display titles, clear bone text on ink, sparse red/cyan emphasis, large interactive targets; avoid persistent CRT overlays. Do not add a public Play CTA until a playable build and URL have been verified.
